# Kumo - The No. 1 Local Shop App 

![Kumo App](kumo-logo.png)

Currently in development, the platform provides local South African small shop owners the ability to open their own 
e-commerce online store attracting people from the area making business easier to do everyday. 

The platform will be available in phases:

- Phase 1: Initial phase showcasing user + store registration, merchants can add products and 
customers can view these stores and products.

- Phase 2: Payment gateways offers customers to purchase products from stores owned by merchants. POS integration will come in at a later stage.

- Phase 3: Statistical data for store owners provided on product sales and marketing using AI and Business Intelligence technologies.

## Kumo POS

The platform will support POS integration via the app and/or using Kumo branded hardware.
